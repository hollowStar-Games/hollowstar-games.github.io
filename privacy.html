Echo · Privacy Policy

Developer: Hollow Star · Last updated: 14 October 2026

Echo is a puzzle game for Android made by Hollow Star ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. Echo has no user accounts and we do not run any servers that collect your data.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, stars, shards, hints, purchased styles and settings are saved on your device (and in your Google account only if you turn on cloud save, below). We cannot see this data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. Android's own backup may include it if you have device backup turned on.

Optional cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Echo saves a copy of your game progress (levels, stars, shards, hints, styles and settings) to your Google account using Google Play Games Saved Games, so you can restore it on another phone. This copy is stored by Google, not by us. Your Play Games profile name is shown in the game. You can sign out or delete Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Daily reminder (optional)

If you turn on the daily reminder, Echo schedules a notification on your phone once a day. This happens entirely on your device; no information is sent to us.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details. Google tells the game which item you bought so it can be added to your game. Google's handling of payment data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Optional video ads

You can choose to watch a short video to earn a free hint. These videos are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads and measure them, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and approximate location. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

Children

Echo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data held by Google, use your Google account settings or contact Google. If you live in the EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
